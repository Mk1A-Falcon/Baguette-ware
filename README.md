# Baguette-Ware
A Marioware Based Web-Playable game on "Baguettes"
All games are based on a ingame timer(themed_timer.tscn and level_scene's inbuilt timer function) Complete all challenged in the given time to win!
<img src="display_readme\image copy.png">
## To play-
Click on [this link](https://mk1a.itch.io/baguette-ware) and click "run game" to play
Additionally check out the "fullscreen" button on the bottom right of the playable window*
<img src="display_readme\image.png">

## List of games-
Other non-mentioned levels are Currently in development*
### 1. Baguette_Platformer 
Use A for Left, D for Right and space for Jump + Arrow keys for movement in their direction. 
Get all 4 baguette_pickups in 6 seconds to pass.
<img src="display_readme/image copy 2.png">
### 2. Baguette_Clicker
Click on each button(baguette) in 5 seconds to pass. 
<img src="display_readme/image copy 3.png">
